# Old Lagos Island (district 9, build b55)

Ninth district of the Lagos world plan. A new part of the world, the Island, is joined to the mainland by a coach from Yaba Terminal and a ferry from the far pier at Makoko. It holds Marina, the Cathedral, Freedom Park, Tafawa Balewa Square, Balogun Market, the old Brazilian quarter, Campos Square and the palace forecourt. It was built and tested on its own before any other district is started.

## What is real and what is invented

Sources (text only, no photographs were available): the Wikipedia article on the Cathedral Church of Christ, the Diapason article on its organs, the Guardian Nigeria feature on Freedom Park, the Discover Lagos page on Tafawa Balewa Square, and the Wikipedia article on the architecture of Lagos.

Confirmed by those sources and used in the game:

- The Cathedral Church of Christ, Marina. Foundation stone of the first building 29 March 1867, established 1869. The present Norman Gothic church was begun 1 November 1924, its stone was laid by the Prince of Wales on 21 April 1925, and it was completed in 1946. Architect Bagan Benjamin. Seat of the Anglican Bishop of Lagos.
- The Oberlinger organ: four manuals, 64 stops, about 3,700 pipes, dedicated 9 May 2010. One source calls it the largest organ in Nigeria.
- Freedom Park stands on the Broad Street Prison site. The prison was built in 1872 and rebuilt in 1885 with bricks from England, and it closed in 1972. The architect Theo Lawson made the park for the fiftieth independence anniversary in October 2010. Wole Soyinka is principal patron. The real park keeps 52 cells and has a museum. Activists such as Obafemi Awolowo were held at Broad Street.
- Tafawa Balewa Square was the Race Course, used for horse racing in colonial times, and was made a ceremonial ground for independence in 1960. It is named after Sir Abubakar Tafawa Balewa. The white horses and red eagles stand for strength, progress and dignity.
- The Afro-Brazilian returnees, called the Aguda, who built houses with verandahs, shutters and decorated fronts on the Island.
- The Shitta-Bey Mosque was completed in 1894 and is credited to the Afro-Brazilian builder Joao Baptista da Costa. It was built for the merchant Mohammed Shitta-Bey and is a national monument.
- Balogun is one of the busiest markets on the Island, known for lace, ankara and ready-made clothes. Balogun is a Yoruba title for a war chief.
- Iga Idunganran is the palace of the Oba of Lagos. The Eyo, also called the Adamu Orisha Play, is a Lagos Island masquerade tradition: white robes, wide hats, staffs, with different hat colours for different groups.
- Lagos State runs public ferries through its waterways authority, LASWA.

Invented, and labelled as invented in the game:

- The layout. The road, the order of the buildings, the compounds, the stall yard, the jetty and the square are not surveyed. Distances are compressed.
- Every shop, stall, hall, room and person: Mama Risikat, Baba Lateef, Engr. Dosunmu, the verger, Miss Ada, Mr Bello, Iya Alamala, Ms Tope, Mr Kola, Mrs Da Silva, Mr Pereira, Mallam Sani, Aunty Shola, the clerks, guards and every walker.
- The insides of all six buildings. The gallery is not the real Freedom Park gallery and the heritage house is not a real museum.
- Opening hours, prices, wages, the ferry route and fare, the coach route and fare, and the coach and ferry times.
- The five Eyo models, the gate at each end of the road and the festival-ground closure.

## What is built

- A new section of the world about 700 m long west of Makoko, with its own road, two traffic lanes and traffic, lamps, poles, bus stops and a gantry reading "Marina. Lagos Island". The east end meets the Third Mainland Bridge side of the lagoon and the west end is closed.
- North side: shops, the Cathedral compound (nave, tower with a stepped spire, lawns, gate), Freedom Park (gate, prison wall remnants, stage, four sculptures, the gallery building), Marina Bank House, the Balogun Market Hall, a yard of about 100 stalls in five rows, the Heritage House, four Afro-Brazilian houses, Campos Square with a fountain, benches and trees, and the palace forecourt with five Eyo models and a palace with two wings.
- South side: the harbour promenade, the island ferry jetty with a ferry tied up, Tafawa Balewa Square (gate, flag poles, four white horses, two red eagles, a rostrum), the Isale Eko buka, the Shitta-Bey Mosque (outside only), and sixteen old Afro-Brazilian houses with arcades, verandahs and hip roofs.
- The harbour water cannot be walked on: every metre of water that is not a deck is a solid, built as 70 merged blocks.
- Six places you can walk into, each with staff, customers, an exit doorman and a closed board outside:
  - Balogun Market Hall (7 AM to 7 PM): lace and ankara, ready-made clothes, electronics.
  - Cathedral (8 AM to 6 PM): a verger, quiet prayer (free, 15 minutes), a candle for 200, the facts, five rows of pews on each side and a model organ.
  - Marina Bank House (8 AM to 4 PM): a teller and customer service. No money is kept here, and the game says so.
  - Isale Eko buka (7 AM to 9 PM): amala, ewedu and gbegiri, with meat, eba and efo riro, bottled water.
  - Wole Soyinka Art Gallery (10 AM to 7 PM): ticket 1,000 for an hour, the Freedom Park facts and a guide.
  - Afro-Brazilian Heritage House (9 AM to 5 PM): a tour for 800, the Aguda and mosque facts, a curator.
- Street people: the ferry clerk, the coach attendant, the palace guard, the Eyo guide, the square guard, the mosque guard, the market women's rep, a suya seller (4 PM to 11 PM) and a corn seller at Campos Square.
- Links to the rest of the world:
  - Coach from the Marina stop to Yaba Terminal for 600 (75 minutes), and the new "Coach to Lagos Island" ticket at Yaba Terminal for 600 (75 minutes).
  - Ferry from the Island jetty to the Makoko far pier for 500 (30 minutes), and "Ferry to Lagos Island" from the Makoko far pier for 500.
  - Phone rides to every new place and person, and gig spots on the Island.
- Careers: lace trader 3,700, church helper 3,200, bank clerk 6,400 (needs Education Level 2), gallery guide 4,800 (needs Education Level 1), amala cook 3,500.
- 32 walkers outside and 19 inside the rooms, all on daily schedules.
- Minimap: two new strips for the Island with the water, jetty, road and buildings.
- World size: the playable width grew from 5,464 m to 6,330 m. The new rooms sit in two new columns past the Makoko rooms.
- Cars cannot reach the Island by road. The Makoko gate still stops them. The coach and the ferry are the ways across.

## Tests (script names in the build pipeline)

- Interiors: no overlap with other rooms, every person reachable and clear of furniture, exit door reachable (six rooms).
- Street paths: every street person and door reachable on foot from the pavement near it; whole pavements on both sides of the road in 80 m legs; the promenade onto the jetty, the square gate to the rostrum, Freedom Park to the gallery, the cathedral compound, the palace forecourt, Campos Square, the Balogun alley and all four yard aisles, the mosque compound.
- Water and ends: nobody can stand on the harbour water; both ends of the road are closed to people; the jetty deck is walkable.
- Hours: each place refuses entry before opening and accepts it when open, shows or hides its closed board, and closes you out at closing time.
- Services: every price, need change and time cost; the cathedral prayer and candle; gallery ticket; heritage tour; buka meals; suya and corn; the sourced facts in every dialog; refusals when too poor or when the seller is not out.
- Jobs and rides: all five shifts start and pay; education gates; every new place is in the ride list; walkers follow the clock.
- Ferry and coach: both directions, price, time cost, landing on a walkable spot, refusals when too poor, rest at night, and the Yaba menu still lists Agege, Oshodi and Lagos Island.
- Traffic: the Island lanes carry cars both ways, cars stay on the Island road, never overlap, and the mainland lanes never cross the Makoko gate.
- Walker routes and roads: no walker route runs through a solid object; no solid blocks the Island carriageway.
- The b52, b53 and b54 tests (signals, the rail crossing, Agege, Yaba, Makoko) were re-run with no violations, and then the whole existing regression suite on the new build.

One bug was found by that regression run: the amala cook career had the same internal name as an Agege career, which broke the Agege shift. It was renamed.

## Known limits

- The Island here is a slice about 700 m long, not the real map. Real Lagos Island is far larger and denser.
- You cannot drive to it. The Makoko gate stops cars, so you arrive by the coach, the ferry or a phone ride. The Island has its own traffic that never leaves it.
- The Shitta-Bey Mosque, the palace and the other houses are outside only.
- The Eyo are five standing models, not a procession.
- Seated poses do not exist yet, so people in the cathedral, the buka and the gallery stand or walk.
- Real photographs were not used. If you send some, the fronts and the layout can be corrected from them.
