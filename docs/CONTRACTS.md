# Contracts: guns, fights and 54 street missions (build b60)

You asked for at least 50 missions with stealing, killing and guns. This build adds a whole crime career on top of the Lagos world: **54 contracts from six invented fixers**, a health bar, five weapons, armour, enemy gangs that fight back, and a Contracts app on the phone.

## What is real and what is invented

Real: the places used as settings (Ladipo and Mushin, Ojuelegba, Oshodi, Otigba, Ikeja and Allen Avenue, Agege, Yaba, Lagos Island and Balogun, Apapa, Ijora and Tin Can, Ajegunle, Mile 2, Festac, Alaba, Okokomaiko). They are the same streets that are already in the game. No new roads or buildings were added.

Invented, all of it: the six fixers (Oga Tunde, Mama Cash, the Engineer, Sarge Bello, Madam Diamond, Ghost), every gang (Black Cobras, Iron Hand Crew, Red Scorpions, hired muscle, street thugs), every target, client, debtor and crime. No real business, organisation or person commits a crime in any briefing; the missions happen on streets and at junctions, markets and gates, not inside real named companies. Nobody on the Task Force can be shot and passers-by cannot be hurt. Only the gang members in a mission can be, plus a debtor if you choose to fight him.

## How it works

- **Phone, Contracts.** Six fixers, each with nine contracts in order. A fixer unlocks when you reach their RESPECT number (0, 15, 45, 85, 140, 210). Each contract pays cash and RESPECT. You accept one from the phone and it starts at once, anywhere on the Lagos mainland.
- **Armoury (inside Contracts).** Iron pipe ₦1,500, pistol ₦9,000, shotgun ₦35,000, rifle ₦90,000, ammunition packs, body vest ₦20,000 (takes 30% off every hit), plate carrier ₦100,000 (takes 50% off), medkit ₦1,200 (heals 60). Everything arrives instantly.
- **Fighting.** FIRE shoots (hold to keep firing), SWAP changes weapon. It aims for you at the nearest enemy. Keyboard: J fires, K swaps. Enemies cannot see through buildings and they walk around walls to find you. They hit less often when you keep moving. Your health comes back by itself a few seconds after the last hit.
- **If you go down.** The contract ends, you wake at the clinic, a fee of up to ₦2,000 is taken, and you lose three hours.
- **Ten kinds of step**, mixed into the missions: go to a place, kill a gang, hold a spot while a job runs, steal a car (sedan, danfo or okada) and deliver it against a clock, pickpocket from behind (he notices if you come from the front), collect a debt (threaten with a loaded gun or beat him), defend a place against waves, escape the Task Force, escort a client with ambushes on the way, and buy a weapon.
- **Stolen cars.** Your own car is put back where it was when the contract ends, however it ends.
- **Police.** Alarms from stolen cars and failed pickpockets call the Task Force. Getting busted fails the contract. Gunfire alone does not call them.
- **While a contract runs** you cannot start a gig, a shift, a fare or Mama Nkechi's job, and nothing random walks up to you.

## Tests

New tests written for this build (all pass):

- Data (8 checks): 54 contracts in six arcs, unique ids, every place exists on the mainland with a free spot and room for the gang, every pickpocket has a walkable lane at least 6 m long, every fixer can be reached by doing the earlier arcs in order (respect thresholds), weapons are always bought before a contract needs them, and every weapon is affordable from earlier pay.
- Playthrough (6 checks): a script plays **all 54 contracts in order from start to finish**, doing each step the way a player must (walking to places, stealing the cars, lifting wallets from behind, answering the debt dialog, holding spots, escorting clients, losing the Task Force). Every one finishes, pays the right cash and respect, and leaves nothing behind (no enemies, no props, own car back, objective HUD hidden). Locking works: arc 2 is shut at 0 respect and each contract needs the one before it. Total respect comes to exactly 324.
- Combat balance: thirteen fights from every tier, six trials each, with a player who just stands and shoots. Nobody dies in the early and middle fights; the last boss fights beat a stand-still player about half the time.
- Behaviour (19 checks): ammo use, no-ammo, wake-up on gunshots, damage per second per weapon (fists < pipe < pistol <= rifle), shotgun strong close and weak far, armour (none, 30%, 50%), weapon swap order, line of sight blocked by a building and enemies walking around it, dying (contract ends, enemies cleared, you wake at the clinic, fee taken, message shown), busted in a stolen car (contract ends, own car returned), delivery time limit, escort failing when the client dies, pickpocket from the front failing, gigs blocked during a contract and the reverse, and respect, finished contracts, weapons and armour surviving a reload.
- Screens (10 checks, with real renders): the Contracts app on the phone, the six fixers and locks, the Armoury (buying a pipe, a pistol with its first rounds, and a vest), accepting a contract closing the phone, the contract HUD, the fire/swap buttons and the health bar in a fight, and a real tap on the FIRE button firing a round.
- Speed (3 checks): the line-of-sight grid builds in 1.9 ms, a contract with eight armed enemies costs 0.08 ms of script per frame, an idle game 0.007 ms.

Old tests re-run on this build: the whole b52 to b59 suites (1,486 checks in all, including the new ones) and the 17 game-wide regression files and three control tests. Everything passes except six checks that are out of date and also fail in b59: they still expect the west end of the Lagos-Badagry road and the Island road to be shut, and a lane bound that grew when the road was extended.


## Honest limits

- Speed on a real Infinix Hot 30i is not measured: only I cannot do that. On this test machine eight armed enemies cost 0.08 ms of script per frame and the contract code costs nothing when no contract runs. The added load is the enemy characters themselves (up to 14 are kept ready, about eight are on screen at once).
- Combat is arcade style: aiming is automatic, there is no cover system, no crouching and no shooting from a car.
- The fights were balanced with a test player who stands still and shoots. The early contracts are easy, the last ones are hard, and the final fight with the Ghost beats a stand-still player about half the time. Armour and moving make it much easier.
- Contracts only run on the Lagos mainland (west of the start area). Going to the Island, Abuja, Port Harcourt or inside a building pauses the contract; leaving the mainland fails it.
- A mission that needs a weapon you cannot yet afford is refused with the price shown. Earn the money with gigs.
- A contract in progress is not saved. Closing the game mid-contract cancels it. Respect, finished contracts, weapons, ammo and armour are saved.
- Mission locations use the existing door positions. Where a street is crowded, a gang may stand a few steps from the spot you expect.
